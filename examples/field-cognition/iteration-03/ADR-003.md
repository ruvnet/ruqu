# ADR 003: Forecast transported change events

## Specification

Iteration 2 diffused absolute state using irregular time while its generator propagated discrete flips by event index. Iteration 3 changes the task to a three second causal forecast under continuous time propagation. The candidate stores bounded local beliefs plus a capped queue of inferred change arrivals.

## Pseudocode

Apply due change arrivals, decay every belief by the stationary hazard, update only the observed sensor with an exact contaminated Gaussian likelihood, detect confident sign changes, schedule their arrival at node zero using the RuVector derived topology distance, and forecast all arrivals within the horizon.

## Architecture

RuVector WASM supplies neighbor lookup. RuQu WASM supplies secondary classical circuit readouts timed end to end with their field update. The strongest classical comparator remains the acceptance reference. A zero transport arm and shuffled topology arm isolate the claimed mechanism.

## Refinement

Every tuned family received nine development evaluations. The selected velocity candidate was substantially worse on development data. It is retained without further tuning so the reserved holdout remains untouched. The protocol and source must be committed before holdout execution.

## Completion

No promotion unless mean paired relative error improves at least ten percent across five holdout seeds and all correctness checks pass. Any failure is retained as a negative result.
