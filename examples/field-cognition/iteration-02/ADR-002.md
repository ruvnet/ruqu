# ADR 002: Evaluate a change point recurrent field with RuVector topology

## Status

Experimental. No promotion is authorized.

## Specification

Iteration 1 underperformed the scalar Bayesian baseline. This iteration tests whether explicit per node evidence, graph diffusion, leakage, and contradiction resets improve delayed evidence integration on a preregistered propagating wave task.

## Pseudocode

For each timestamped observation, reject malformed or stale input, diffuse the bounded log odds field over a RuVector derived ring, apply exponential leakage, reset locally on strong contradictory evidence, and produce the probability at node zero. Compare on untouched seeds against memoryless, scalar EMA, scalar Bayes, independent Bayes, and the iteration 1 field.

## Architecture

The published `@ruvector/wasm` adapter supplies spatial neighbor lookup. It reports a flat index in this version. `@ruvector/ruqu` supplies a one qubit classical simulation as a secondary contextual readout. The recurrent field and generator are local experimental code. RuField, MidStream, LatentMesh, Autogenous, and MetaHarness remain source audited or unsupported, not executed integrations.

## Refinement

The selected configuration was chosen from 135 configurations on five development seeds. The selection and five new holdout seeds were frozen in `protocol.json` before holdout execution. Fault tests cover replay, malformed data, divergence, missing observations, reset, and deterministic replay.

## Completion rule

Accept only if mean relative decision error improves by at least 10 percent against the strongest comparator over all five holdout seeds and correctness tests pass. A failure remains a publishable negative result and must not be promoted.
